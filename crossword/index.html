SOLVING CONTROLS

  • Click / Tap: Selects a cell and sets the active word. Click the same cell again to toggle direction between Across and Down.
  • Keyboard Inputs [A-Z]: Enters letters into cells and auto-advances the cursor in the active direction.
  • Backspace: Deletes the current cell's letter and moves the cursor backward.
  • Arrow Keys: Navigate freely between intersecting grid spaces.
  • Space / Enter: Rotate word input direction between Across and Down.
  • Tab / Shift+Tab: Jump to the next or previous clue in sequence.
